Anming Gu

I'm a second-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science at UT Austin, where I am fortunate to be advised by Kevin Tian. Previously, I completed my B.A. in Computer Science at Boston University, where I worked with Ed Chien and Kristjan Greenewald on optimal transport for machine learning.

I primarily work on logconcave sampling and data privacy. More broadly, I'm interested in problems at the intersection of theoretical computer science, high-dimensional statistics, probability theory, and machine learning. Recently, I've also been thinking about the theoretical foundations of generative models, along with AI safety and alignment.

In summer 2026, I interned at A*STAR in Singapore, hosted by Atsushi Nitanda.

Please feel free to reach out if you're interested in my work!
